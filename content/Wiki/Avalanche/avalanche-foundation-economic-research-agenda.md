# Avalanche Foundation economic research agenda

The Foundation's July 30, 2026 agenda defines one organizing problem: how economic value created across Avalanche connects to value accruing to AVAX. It proposes a three-stage research framework and three matching research streams.

## Measure, Capture, Distribute

1. **Measure** ecosystem production and income using GCP, GCI, and the channels through which value moves.
2. **Capture** the portion that accrues to AVAX today and evaluate sustainable protocol-level capture mechanisms.
3. **Distribute** captured value between AVAX and ecosystem recipients according to explicit objectives.

The framework is a research directive, not a finding. Every proposed connection still needs definitions, data, mechanisms, counterfactuals, and tests.

## Research streams

- **Tokenomics and protocol revenue capture** studies fees, MEV, application and L1 revenue sharing, and other protocol-native mechanisms without assuming capture is costless.
- **Validator economics and long-term staking incentives** studies continuous security funding and the transition from issuance toward fee revenue.
- **Ecosystem capital allocation** studies burns, validator-directed revenue, and other distributions intended to support AVAX value accrual.

## Phase II use

The staking research sits directly in the second stream but depends on all three:

- measurement defines the economic and security baselines;
- capture determines the revenue available to support security;
- distribution determines how much reaches validators and through which mechanism;
- validator and delegator responses determine the resulting security and participation outcomes.

The agenda therefore supplies the study hierarchy, while the validator-rewards article supplies one behavioral model candidate. Neither source substitutes for independent data, reproduction, or model validation.
