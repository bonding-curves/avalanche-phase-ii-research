This site holds the research BCRG is carrying out for the Avalanche Foundation under the Phase II agreement. The question is how validator and delegator behaviour, the reward design, and protocol changes such as Helicon together determine the network's economic security. Every page of the site maps to a deliverable in the agreement, so the site shows at a glance what is delivered and what is still to come.

## Deliverables and their status

The research follows the schedule of proposal v2.5. Milestone 1 was rebuilt from the chain up in September and is being delivered again inside Milestone 2's window.

## Where to start

1. **The latest call.** The [2026-10-02 call page](Phase%20II/Calls/2026-10-02/) has the agenda and what we did about each of the Foundation's comments on the last one.
2. **The data.** [Data](Reference/Data) explains the 35 datasets, where each comes from, and how every number traces to a checked run. Deliverables [1.1](Phase%20II/Milestone%201/1-1%20Data%20Ingestion/) and [1.2](Phase%20II/Milestone%201/1-2%20Data%20Manifest/) list them in full.
3. **The notebooks.** [Notebooks](Reference/Notebooks) describes the two notebooks you can run: the evidence behind *How Do Avalanche Validators Actually Think About Staking Rewards?*, and every staking commitment since genesis.
4. **The vocabulary.** [Glossary and Notation](Reference/Glossary%20and%20Notation) defines every term and symbol the site uses.

## Running it yourself

This site, the notebooks, the code, and the data all come from one repository. `SETUP.md` at its root takes a new machine from cloning to running notebooks, step by step, and an AI coding agent can follow it as written.
