# Avalanche Phase II

Phase II develops evidence and software for reasoning about Avalanche staking economics. The central concern is how validator and delegator participation, reward design, liquidity, issuance, protocol changes, and market conditions interact with economic security and long-term network sustainability.

The research moves from sources and observed data to independent reproduction, dynamic modeling, uncertainty analysis, and decision support. The current site establishes the source and milestone foundation. Most empirical and simulation results remain under development.

## Study structure

Each admitted research node separates three questions:

1. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** What does the available data contain, and what can it support?
2. **Reproduction:** Can published quantities, figures, formulas, or protocol mechanics be independently reconstructed?
3. **Dynamic Model:** What changes when participant behavior, system state, governance choices, and external conditions interact through time?

A study may mark a component blocked or not applicable. An empty page, rendered notebook, or successful software test is not a research result.

## Current research node

The first initialized study is [How Do Avalanche Validators Actually Think About Staking Rewards?](Studies/Essential%20Readings/How%20Do%20Avalanche%20Validators%20Actually%20Think%20About%20Staking%20Rewards/index.md). It examines the article's validator and delegator choice models, reported policy counterfactuals, required data, and possible dynamic extensions.

Its [source article](Studies/Essential%20Readings/How%20Do%20Avalanche%20Validators%20Actually%20Think%20About%20Staking%20Rewards/Source%20Article.md) is preserved inside the study package. Source fidelity does not establish empirical validity.

## Evidence path

```text
Essential Readings and protocol sources
  -> claims, definitions, formulas, and open questions
  -> Avalanche data and exploratory analysis
  -> independent reproduction
  -> cadCAD mechanisms and dynamic behavior
  -> scenarios, KPIs, PSUU, sensitivity, and risk regions
  -> findings, recommendations, playbook, and handoff
```
