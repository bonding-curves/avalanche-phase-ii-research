A brief is the pre-read for one call. It has three parts: takeaways, each linked to its memo, discussion items that each end in a question for the Foundation, and next steps with owners and dates. It is shared with the Foundation before the call. Briefs in [drafts](/Phase-II/Wiki/Briefs/drafts/) have not been submitted yet.
