---
title: Memos
description: "One finding per memo, chart first, named by date and finding."
---

A memo carries one finding. It opens with the chart, states the finding in two sentences with its number, unit, comparison, and source run, says what it means for a decision, shows how it was built, and names what is open. Memos in [drafts](/Phase-II/Wiki/Memos/drafts/) have not been submitted yet.

The [research history](/Phase-II/Wiki/Research-History/) preserves earlier work in its original form. Its first methods note and work plan are historical editions, with links to the October model explanation.
