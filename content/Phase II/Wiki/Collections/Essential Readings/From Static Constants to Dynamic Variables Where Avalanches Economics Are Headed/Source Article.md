# From Static Constants to Dynamic Variables: Where Avalanche's Economics Are Headed

Matias Antonio published the [author edition](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/from-static-constants-dynamic-variables-where-avalanches-matias-a-xh02e) on July 2, 2026. This page is a study guide, not a copy of the article. Its descriptions of ACP status are publication-time statements, not a current protocol-status register.

## What the article connects

The article discusses shorter validator commitments under ACP-273, a lower short-duration reward floor under ACP-285, and an adjustable C-Chain minimum gas price under ACP-283. Its larger argument is that economic parameters should be evaluated against explicit objectives and changing network conditions rather than treated as permanent launch constants. It also distinguishes a reward-formula parameter from the effective rate a validator receives after the remaining-supply factor is applied.

## Why it belongs in the reading sequence

Read this after the [validator-rewards article](/Phase-II/Wiki/Collections/Essential-Readings/How-Do-Avalanche-Validators-Actually-Think-About-Staking-Rewards/) and before the [ACP-285 community call](/Phase-II/Wiki/Collections/Essential-Readings/ACP-285-Avalanche-Community-Call-Staking-Reward-Curve-Adjustment/). It connects participant-choice hypotheses to separately specified protocol levers. A source's expectation about adoption or duration is not an observed outcome.

For Milestone 1, verify each rule and activation date against protocol evidence and observed staking behavior. For Milestone 2, model duration bounds, reward-curve parameters, and fee-floor governance as distinct, switchable mechanisms, with explicit interaction experiments and measured outcomes.
