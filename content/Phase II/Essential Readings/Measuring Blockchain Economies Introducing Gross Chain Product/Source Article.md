# Measuring Blockchain Economies: Introducing Gross Chain Product

Eric Lu published the [author edition](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/measuring-blockchain-economies-introducing-gross-chain-lu-phd-juj3c) on May 7, 2026. This page is a study guide, not a copy of the article or an independent reproduction of its figures.

## What the article introduces

Gross Chain Product (GCP) is an economy-wide measure of on-chain value added. The article builds a C-Chain example using an income approach and distinguishes nominal output, valued at current prices, from real output adjusted with Fisher chain-weighting. It argues that token market capitalization, total value locked, and one protocol's revenue answer different questions from ecosystem production.

## Why it belongs in the reading sequence

Read this before [From Gross Chain Product to Gross Chain Income](/Phase-II/Essential-Readings/From-Gross-Chain-Product-to-Gross-Chain-Income-Where-the-Value-Goes/). The later article extends the accounting boundary to income received by ecosystem residents and income retained by external issuers. Neither article implies that measured production already reaches AVAX holders or pays validators.

For Milestone 1, reconstruct the article's value-added definition, population, observation period, price treatment, and reported quantities from identifiable inputs. For Milestone 2, keep production, potential capture, actual capture, and distribution as separate quantities before testing a mechanism that might connect them.
