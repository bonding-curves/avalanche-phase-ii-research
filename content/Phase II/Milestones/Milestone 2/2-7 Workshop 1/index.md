## Status

Scheduled. The Milestone 2 overview is on the Foundation's call on Friday 30 October 2026. Workshop 1 is on Friday 13 November 2026.

## Result

The overview on 30 October walks through the model, the method and the results of the [Sensitivity Analysis Report](/Phase-II/Milestones/Milestone-2/Sensitivity-Analysis-Report/), delivered two days earlier so the Foundation can read it first.

Workshop 1 on 13 November covers the whole model, the results and the goals, and it collects the Foundation's answers to five questions that the recommendations of Milestone 3 depend on.

1. Is the purpose of the reward rule to buy the most security for each AVAX issued, within floors on security and participation?
2. What value should the cost of an attack exceed: the assets on the C-Chain, a day's settled value, or AVAX's market value?
3. Should net inflation be measured on total supply or on circulating supply, and is 5% a year a hard ceiling?
4. How much validator stake would the Foundation give up to issue one million fewer AVAX a year?
5. What does it cost a year to run a validator, and which outside return should a validator's reward be compared with?

The workshop also presents the result of the held-back test of deliverable [2.4](/Phase-II/Milestones/Milestone-2/2-4-Validation-Confirmation/). It asks the Foundation to rank how much it values each goal of deliverable [2.3](/Phase-II/Milestones/Milestone-2/2-3-KPI-Computation/), which sets the weights the recommendations are scored against.

## Next step

The workshop materials, a record of the session and the Foundation's feedback are published here after 13 November. The floors and weights set at the workshop carry into Milestone 3.
