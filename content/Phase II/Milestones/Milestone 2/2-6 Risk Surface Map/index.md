## Result

When delivered, this page will show a map of the governance parameter space with the failing regions marked, then explain which KPI thresholds fail in each region and under which scenarios. The proposal counts a configuration as failing when any KPI crosses its threshold, and it tests failure under high volatility, sudden shocks, and sustained stress such as a bear market with high external yields.
