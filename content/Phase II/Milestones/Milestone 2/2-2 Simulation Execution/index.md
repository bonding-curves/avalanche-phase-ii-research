## Result

When delivered, this page will show a table of the completed sweep, with its governance configurations, environmental scenarios, and Monte Carlo runs, then explain what the sweep covers. The proposal sizes the sweep at 500 governance configurations, 20 scenarios, and 5 runs each, which is 50,000 trajectories. Its scenarios vary external yields from 3% to 10%, market conditions across bull, neutral, and bear, and network growth across low, medium, and high.
