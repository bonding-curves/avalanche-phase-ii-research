Milestone 2 tests reward designs across conditions to measure how each one changes participation, security and issuance. Its eight deliverables are delivered through the [Sensitivity Analysis Report](/Phase-II/Milestones/Milestone-2/Sensitivity-Analysis-Report/), delivered on 28 October 2026, whose Appendix A maps each deliverable to the section that delivers it. The full overview is presented on 30 October 2026, and Workshop 1 on 13 November 2026 closes the milestone.

**Signed agreement period:** Months 3 to 4, June 15 to August 14, 2026.

**Working period:** August 15 to October 14, 2026, under the schedule of proposal v2.5, with the report delivered on 28 October and Workshop 1 on 13 November 2026. The signed agreement's dates are shown for reference.

**Focus:** PSUU implementation, Monte Carlo sweeps, and sensitivity analysis

**Contract success criterion:** Sensitivity Analysis Report delivered covering all key parameters. Delivered on 28 October 2026.
