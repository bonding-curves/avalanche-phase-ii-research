## Result

When delivered, this page will show a chart of simulated against observed staking for 2025, then a table of goodness-of-fit metrics against their targets. The proposal's test starts the model from the observed state in January 2025, simulates through December 2025, and compares the result with what happened. Its targets are correlation above 0.90, a staking ratio error under 2 percentage points, trend direction matched more than 85% of the time, and changes timed within 7 days. Deliverable 2.4 repeats this test on the final model.
