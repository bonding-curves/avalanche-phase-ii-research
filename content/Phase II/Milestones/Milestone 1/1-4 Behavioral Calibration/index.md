## Status

## Result

When delivered, this page will show a table of the fitted stake, unstake, and restake response functions with each parameter's estimate and confidence interval, then explain the fit. The proposal fits these functions to 2024 and 2025 staking history and checks them on data held out from the fit. It treats its illustrative tanh form as one candidate and lets the data choose among forms such as logistic and piecewise linear.
