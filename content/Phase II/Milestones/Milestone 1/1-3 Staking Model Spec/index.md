## Status

In progress as of 2026-09-28.

## Result

When delivered, this page will show a table of the model's state variables, governance parameters, and update rules, then explain how stake moves from one time step to the next. The proposal names the governance parameters as the minimum and maximum commitment length, the yield at each length, and the validator's share of rewards. It names three behavioral state variables: the stake rate, which responds to the gap between staking yield and external yields, the unstake rate, which responds to external yields, and the restake rate, which responds to the gain from compounding.
