## Status

Summary published on September 28, 2026. The [evidence analysis](/Phase-II/Interviews/BENQI-2026-08-31/Analysis) and [interview method](/Phase-II/Interviews/Interview-Method) were published on October 1, 2026. The full transcript is withheld pending Hansen's consent.

## Summary

These points summarize Hansen's account. The linked analysis checks selected delegation observations and retains the limits of attribution, coverage and interpretation.

1. BENQI's liquid staking delegations are commitments of 14 to 18 days, a length set for its users' liquidity.
2. BENQI allocates these delegations with bots that weigh each validator's capacity, uptime, duration, and stake.
3. BENQI holds 10% to 12% of all stake.
4. Before Helicon, Hansen described a possible move toward two-day commitments after the upgrade, with an implementation delay.
5. The minimum delegation commitment is still 14 days, so BENQI could shorten its commitments only through validators it runs itself.
