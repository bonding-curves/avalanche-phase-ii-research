This Grant Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of 15 April 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Avalanche (BVI), Inc., a company formed under the laws of the British Virgin Islands ("Company"), Longtail Financial Corp. a Corporation organized under the laws of Canada ("Developer," and, together with the Company, the "Parties" and each, a "Party").

WHEREAS, the Developer will undertake certain activities as described on Exhibit A ("Activities") that will engage users and encourage the adoption of the Avalanche public blockchain; and

WHEREAS, to encourage the undertaking of those Activities, the Company wishes to provide the Grant (as defined below) to the Developer.

In consideration of the premises above, the mutual promises below and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Grant. Company agrees to make available a grant of up to the Total Grant Amount, as set forth on Table A, to Developer (the "Grant") in accordance with Exhibit A.

2. Unused Grant. Developer shall return to Company, promptly upon request of Company or immediately upon the Termination Date (as defined below), any portion of the Grant that has not been used in accordance with this Agreement, regardless of the reasons it was not so used.

3. Operation of Validator Node This Section 3 is only applicable if Item 3 in Table A above is marked "Yes"). During the term of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year after its termination, Developer shall operate at least one validator node on the Avalanche public blockchain.

4. Information and Reports. Each Party shall maintain information about the Activities and its performance of the Activities. Each Party shall, upon the reasonable request of the other Party, provide relevant information and reports about the Activities, as well as progress and results. Without limiting the foregoing, Developer shall maintain information about its usage of the Grant and any unused amounts of the Grant.
