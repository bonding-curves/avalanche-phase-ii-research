⚠️ Why We CANNOT Rely on Asking BENQI to Voluntarily Lengthen Lockups

BENQI is a liquid staking protocol issuer (sAVAX). Their core value proposition is liquidity, instant redemption buffers, and dynamic rebalancing across high-performing validators. Asking BENQI to voluntarily lock their delegations into 60-day or 90-day terms forces them to take on severe duration risk and eliminates their liquidity reserves. They have zero incentive to agree to this. Therefore, the protocol cannot rely on off-chain cooperation; the on-chain mechanism itself ($S_{\min}(d)$ combined with ACP-285 yield curve steepening) must make longer commitments strictly profit-maximizing for validators and delegators alike.

The 6,000 AVAX Threshold Step Function: BENQI's Open Pool (65% of volume) requires validator self-stake $\ge 6{,}000 ext{ AVAX}$, a fee of exactly $2\%$, and uptime $\ge 15 ext{ days}$. On-chain analysis proves that crossing from 5,999 to 6,000 AVAX self-bond increases expected delegation volume by $1.53 imes$ locally and $80 imes$ across the whole network. Validators with $\ge 6{,}000 ext{ AVAX}$ captured $97.18\%$ of all delegation volume in network history ($2.67 ext{B AVAX}$). Any admission frontier must preserve this knot at $(14 ext{d}, 6{,}000 ext{ AVAX})$ to avoid breaking physical node financing.

📐 4. Mathematical Mechanics & Governing Equations

1. Global Sybil Parity Invariant:

To prevent stake-splitting attacks that dilute consensus sampling or bloat P-Chain state, the cumulative capital-duration commitment at any duration $d$ must equal or exceed Mainnet's canonical baseline ($2{,}000\text{ AVAX} \times 14\text{ days} = 28{,}000\text{ AVAX-days}$):

$$\kappa(d) = S_{\min}(d) \cdot d \ge 28{,}000\text{ AVAX-days} \implies S_{\min}(2\text{d}) \ge 14{,}000\text{ AVAX}$$

2. Harmonic Bounded Decay Curve:

$$S_{\min}(d) = 500 + (S_{\text{short}} - 500) \cdot \left[ \frac{\frac{1}{d} - \frac{1}{365}}{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{365}} \right]^p$$ $$\text{where }\quad p = \frac{\ln\left(\frac{2{,}000 - 500}{S_{\text{short}} - 500}\right)}{\ln\left(\frac{\frac{1}{d_{\text{target}}} - \frac{1}{365}}{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{365}}\right)}$$

3. Piecewise-BENQI Curve (4-Knot Inverse-Linear Interpolation):

Pinned explicitly to four structural market coordinates: $(2\text{d}, S_{\text{short}})$, $(14\text{d}, 6{,}000\text{ AVAX})$, $(d_{\text{target}}, 2{,}000\text{ AVAX})$, and $(365\text{d}, 500\text{ AVAX})$. Between any two knots $(d_0, s_0)$ and $(d_1, s_1)$, admission stake interpolates linearly in inverse-time space:

$$t(d) = \frac{\frac{1}{d} - \frac{1}{d_1}}{\frac{1}{d_0} - \frac{1}{d_1}}, \quad S_{\min}(d) = s_1 + (s_0 - s_1) \cdot t(d)$$
