📊 Plot 1: Retail Node Retention (Top Left)

What it measures: The percentage of the 6,440 physical retail validators that remain active after 1 full year (52 weeks = 364 days).

How to read it:

  • X-axis ($S_{\text{short}}$): The minimum stake required at $d = 2\text{ days}$ ($14\text{k}$, $16\text{k}$, or $20\text{k AVAX}$).
  • Y-axis ($d_{\text{target}}$): The minimum lockup duration required for a $2{,}000\text{ AVAX}$ validator ($6\text{w}$ to $16.9\text{w}$).
  • Why it's ~40%–54% (not 100%): Unlike bare-metal nodes with sunk costs, retail validators run on cloud VPS ($40/mo = -0.96\%$ APY drag). Their outside option is sAVAX liquid staking ($4.15\%$ frictionless yield). If uncompensated by delegations, operators rationally shut down their VPS and hold sAVAX upon expiration. Routing delegation matching preserves node retention above $50\%$.

📈 Plot 2: Retail Duration Lift (Top Right)

What it measures: The percentage increase in average staking duration chosen by retail validators compared to the empirical baseline ($37.1\text{ days}$ from the Mainnet renewal panel).

How to read it:

  • Empirical Reality: On Mainnet today, $71.6\%$ of $2{,}000\text{ AVAX}$ validators choose $14\text{d}$ or $30\text{d}$ stints.
  • Causal Mechanism: When $S_{\min}(d)$ blocks short $14\text{d}$ stints for $2{,}000\text{ AVAX}$, stakers cluster at the shortest legally admissible duration ($d_{\text{target}}$).
  • Interpretation: At $d_{\text{target}} = 8\text{w}$ ($56\text{d}$), retail duration increases to $\sim 52\text{d} - 62\text{d}$ (a +40% to +66% lift). At $16.9\text{w}$ ($118\text{d}$), it lifts by +80%.

⚖️ Plot 3: Stake-Weighted Duration (Bottom Left)

What it measures: The average commitment duration across the entire network, weighted by capital (AVAX).

How to read it:

  • Whale Invariant: Institutional stakers ($\ge 6{,}000\text{ AVAX}$) control $>98\%$ of all staked capital. Their decisions dominate the macro security horizon.
  • ACP-285 Yield Curve Steepening: Under ACP-285, ARR rises from $4.08\%$ at $14\text{d}$ to $6.38\%$ at $365\text{d}$. Large holders shift out of $14\text{d}-30\text{d}$ churn into $90\text{d}-180\text{d}$ commitments to capture $+230\text{ bps}$ of yield.
  • Macro Horizon: Across all parameter regimes, capital-weighted duration stabilizes between $19.7\text{ weeks}$ and $23.9\text{ weeks}$ ($\sim 5\text{ months}$), more than doubling Mainnet's empirical $11.6\text{-week}$ baseline.

📉 Plot 4: 52-Week Trajectories & The Week 25 Dip (Bottom Right)

What it measures: The continuous week-by-week trajectory of stake-weighted network duration from Week 1 to Week 52.

Why does the curve drop at Week 25–26?

  • The 6-Month Expiration Cliff: Avalanche initial state contains whale cohorts with $180\text{-day}$ ($25.7\text{-week}$) commitments.
  • Rollover Wave: At Week 25–26, this large tranche of capital matures simultaneously. When they re-enter and re-evaluate their liquidity horizon, the network-wide average temporarily dips from $\sim 22\text{w}$ to $\sim 17.5\text{w}-19.5\text{w}$.
  • Recovery: As ACP-285 yield incentives take effect, renewed capital rolls back into longer commitments, lifting the trajectory back toward $\sim 21\text{w}-24\text{w}$ by Week 52.
