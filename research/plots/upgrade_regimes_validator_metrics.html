🔍 Key Architectural Observations Across Regimes
  • The Durango Shift: Unweighted median commitment held remarkably steady between 4.1 and 5.0 weeks from Genesis all the way through Cortina. At Durango (March 2024), it collapsed by half to exactly 2.14 weeks (15.0 days) and remained frozen there through Etna and Fortuna. Why? Durango moved the start of a staking period to its exact transaction acceptance time, eliminating the padding wallets used to add.
  • The Granite Stake Surge: The Granite regime (late 2025 – Sept 2026) differed in stake rather than duration. Median self-stake per period surged to 14,585 AVAX (compared to ~2,000 AVAX in every previous regime), while the stake-weighted median duration hit its historical low of 3.97 weeks.
  • Apricot Phase 6: The three short Apricot Phase 6 sub-regimes hold only 832 periods total across 6 days and represent emergency client hardfork fixes.
