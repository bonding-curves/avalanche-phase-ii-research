🎯 1. Ecosystem-First Macro Goals

The mathematical architecture of the Admission Frontier is designed strictly to serve three interconnected ecosystem goals:

  • 1. Prevent 48-Hour Saturation (Sybil Parity): With ACP-273 activating a 2-day minimum staking duration, the network cannot allow historical 14-day churn capital to collapse into 48-hour stints at no cost. The frontier must mathematically guarantee that 2-day staking requires proportionally higher capital commitment.
  • 2. Validator Maximization & Profitability: Decentralization is not strictly defined by the "2,000 AVAX" node, but by maximizing the total number of independent validators. The frontier must gently guide capital toward longer lockups where ACP-285 yields safely outpace sovereign hardware costs (VPS drag), ensuring independent nodes remain profitable.
  • 3. Gradual Macro Duration Expansion: The goal is to lift the network's capital lock duration across the board without causing aggressive liquidity shocks. We provide flexible parameter ranges rather than rigid dictates, allowing capital to adjust organically.

📐 3. The Continuous Harmonic Frontier

We strongly recommend the Continuous Harmonic Bounded Decay curve. It treats all ecosystem capital fairly, applying a smooth, continuous mathematical function without arbitrary "steps" or accommodations for specific third-party smart contracts.

$$S_{\min}(d) = 500 + (S_{\text{short}} - 500) \cdot \left[ \frac{\frac{1}{d} - \frac{1}{365}}{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{365}} \right]^p, \quad p = \frac{\ln\left(\frac{2{,}000 - 500}{S_{\text{short}} - 500}\right)}{\ln\left(\frac{\frac{1}{d_{\text{target}}} - \frac{1}{365}}{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{365}}\right)}$$

The Genius of the Harmonic Curve for 14-Day Capital: The heavy concentration of capital churning at 14 days currently resides in nodes with $>6,000$ AVAX. Under a moderate Harmonic envelope, a 14-day lockup mathematically requires $\sim 4,500$ to $7,000$ AVAX. Therefore, this capital is not violently evicted—they already have the stake to maintain their 14-day cadence. The curve simply acts as a wall, preventing them from dropping to 2 days without putting up $14,000+$ AVAX.
