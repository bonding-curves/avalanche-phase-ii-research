📄 Formal Engineering Memo & Theoretical Mechanics

1. The Problem with 2D Heatmaps in Parameter Selection

Bivariate heatmaps ($S_{\text{short}}$ vs. $d_{\text{target}}$) force an arbitrary 2D slice through a complex dynamic system. In reality, the admission frontier operates in a high-dimensional space spanned by:

  • Outside Yield Invariant: Frictionless liquid staking returns on sAVAX ($4.15\% - 4.50\%$ APR).
  • VPS Operational Drag: Fixed hardware costs ($\sim \$40/\text{month}$ VPS overhead imposes a $-0.96\%$ yield drag on a $2{,}000\text{ AVAX}$ validator).
  • Asynchronous Rollover Arrivals: Stakers enter and mature continuously over time ($U(1, d_{\text{choice}})$), preventing artificial synchronized rollover shockwaves.

By adopting the Subspace Network trajectory/utility tensor methodology, we eliminate visual guesswork. We simulate the entire combinatorial grid, project the trajectories into objective KPI vectors, and apply a binary utility function $U(\theta) \in \{0, 1\}$ to isolate the safe envelope.

2. Mechanism Comparison: Piecewise-BENQI vs. Harmonic Hyperbolic

The tensor sweep revealed a clear operational divergence between the two candidate curves:

  • Piecewise-BENQI (Flat Tiered Admission): By isolating retail validators ($2{,}000\text{ AVAX}$) at a flat step of $8\text{ to }10\text{ weeks}$, small operators are shielded from steep marginal derivative penalties. This yields early admissibility starting at $d=56\text{d}$ ($8\text{w}$) and $S_{\text{short}}=10{,}000\text{ AVAX}$, achieving $51.8\%$ node retention.
  • Harmonic Hyperbolic Curve ($S_{\min}(d) = S_{\text{base}} \cdot (d_{\max} / d)^\alpha$): Because the hyperbolic curve continuously penalizes shorter horizons, retail validators must be pushed to $84\text{d}$ ($12\text{w}$) before the retention gate passes ($52.7\%$). However, when operators commit to longer horizons ($d=118\text{d}$), the harmonic curve produces the highest overall node retention ($59.0\%$) and maximum network duration ($23.87\text{ weeks}$) because high yields completely dominate VPS hosting drag.
