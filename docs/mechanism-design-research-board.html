$$\frac{d S_{\min}(d)}{dd} < 0 \quad \forall d \in [d_{\min}, d_{\max}]$$

$$S(d_{\min}) = S_{\text{short}}, \quad S(d_{\max}) = S_{\text{floor}}$$

5. Candidate Curves

$$S_1(d) = \max\left(S_{\text{floor}}, \, \frac{K_{\text{base}}}{d}\right)$$

Fatal Flaw: Flattens completely at day 56. Zero duration incentive from 2 months to 1 year.

Family 2
Standard Hyperbolic
$$S_2(d) = S_{\text{floor}} + \frac{S_{\text{short}} - S_{\text{floor}}}{1 + \alpha(d - d_{\min})}$$

Fatal Flaw: Heavy tail overshoots $S_{\text{floor}}$ by >50% at 365d.

Family 3
Power-Law Decay
$$S_3(d) = S_{\text{floor}} + \Delta S \left(\frac{d_{\max} - d}{d_{\max} - d_{\min}}\right)^p$$

Fatal Flaw: Requires $p > 6$, creating severe hockey-stick distortion at 14d.

Family 4 (Selected)
Bounded Harmonic Decay
$$S_4(d) = S_{\text{floor}} + \Delta S \left[ \frac{\frac{1}{d} - \frac{1}{d_{\max}}}{\frac{1}{d_{\min}} - \frac{1}{d_{\max}}} \right]^p$$

Optimal: Exact boundaries; matches $1/d$ turnover kernel; stable $p \approx 0.65$.
